    Video calls for families far apart

    Farnear is a free video call app for families who live in different countries. A call is a link: send it, and the others open it in a browser or in the Android app. No account and no sign up.

    What you get

    • Video and sound go straight between the devices, encrypted. No server keeps them.
    • A shared board to draw on together, over a photo, a video, a PDF or slides.
    • Several ways to connect. When two networks can't reach each other, a relay passes the encrypted data on.
    • Works in a browser on a phone or a computer, and in the Android app, in 9 languages.

    Questions

    Is Farnear free?
    Yes. A few ads on the pages around a call pay for it, and a call shows none.
    Do I need an account?
    No. Open the link and type your name. Your contacts and rooms stay on your device.
    Who can see my call?
    Only the people with the link. The setup messages are encrypted with the key in the link, so the servers on the way can't read them.
